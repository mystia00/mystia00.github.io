> DLC 3 스토리 스포일러 있음.

## 꿀팁 요약

- **우리 편은 코스즈 고정.** 콤보당 조리 시간 1% 감소라 콤보 10~20만 쌓아도 상대보다 늦게 시작해도 먼저 완성해서 손님 뺏음
- **콤보만 안 끊기면 누구든 압살.** 콤보 끊는 실수가 유일한 패배 원인
- 아운은 원거리 서빙이 없어서 최약. 쓴다면 **토지코전 버림패**로 (석상이라 번개 면역)
- 2승 후 3라운드 제안 등장. 승패와 무관하니 해도 되고 안 해도 됨

**참가 조건**: 명련사·신령묘 전원 인연 5레벨 → 마미조와 대화. 신령묘 요리대회 / 명련사 음악대회 중 선택 (이 페이지는 요리대회).

## 규칙

- 신령묘 3명 vs 우리 3명, **1:1 대결 3판 2선승**
- 평소처럼 영업하는데 **손님을 상대와 공유**. 영업 끝났을 때 점수 높은 쪽 승리
- 재료·음료 100개씩 지급, 메뉴는 명련사·신령묘 인연 요리 고정

| 캐릭터 | 능력 | 평가 |
|---|---|---|
| 모토오리 코스즈 | 보통 이상 평가마다 분위기 +15, 콤보당 조리 시간 1% 감소, 원거리 서빙 | **최강. 고정 픽** |
| 미스티아 | 도철의 만찬, 원거리 서빙, 인내심 프리즈 | 평소랑 같음. 무난 |

> Spoilers for the DLC 3 story.

## Quick tips

- **Always pick Kosuzu.** Each combo cuts cooking time by 1%, so with a 10–20 combo she finishes first even when starting later, and steals the guests
- **Keep the combo going and you crush anyone.** Breaking the combo is the only way to lose
- Aunn has no ranged serving, so she's the weakest. If you use her, **spend her on the Tojiko match** (she's a statue, so lightning can't hurt her)
- After 2 wins you're offered a 3rd round. It doesn't affect the result, so it's up to you

**How to enter**: bond Lv 5 with everyone at Myouren Temple and Divine Spirit Mausoleum → talk to Mamizou. Choose the Mausoleum cooking contest or the Myouren music contest (this page covers the cooking contest).

## Rules

- 3 from the Mausoleum vs our 3, **1-on-1, best of 3**
- You run the shop as usual but **share guests with the opponent**. Higher score at closing wins
- 100 of each ingredient and drink provided. The menu is locked to Myouren Temple and Mausoleum bond dishes

## Our side

| Character | Abilities | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Kosuzu Motoori | Climax +15 per Normal-or-better rating, cooking time −1% per combo, ranged serving | **Strongest. Always pick** |
| Mystia | Taotie's Feast, ranged serving, Patience Freeze | Same as usual. Solid |
| Aunn Komano | Tips +15%, guests you served keep tipping (2× during fever), Patience Freeze | No ranged serving, so the weakest. Immune to lightning |

## Counters per opponent

| Opponent | Disruption | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Seiga Kaku | Locks 3 tables for 20 s every 30 s, walks through walls, Chinese dishes finish instantly | **The weakest.** Moves slowly. Serve unlocked tables first, then throw the moment the lock ends. Even Aunn wins |
| Soga no Tojiko | Lightning on your spot every 10 s (a hit stuns for 10 s; up to 4 bolts as you dodge), 50% chance to teleport, pot dishes finish instantly | Dodge the moment the floor warning shows. It also hits the kitchen, so watch out while cooking. Teleporting steals every guest unless you have ranged serving |
| Mononobe no Futo | Fire on 3 spots every 20 s (touching it slows you for 5 s + reverses controls), cutting-board dishes finish instantly, throws dishes and drinks | Move around the fire. She's fast because she throws → win on cooking speed by keeping the combo |
